 SIH Technical Architecture & Proof

The Core Challenge: Smartphone accelerometers experience double-integration quadratic position drift ($x = \frac{1}{2} a_{bias} t^2$). A $0.02\text{ m/s}^2$ bias causes $> 50\text{ m}$ lateral error in 40 seconds.

Defensible 4-Pillar Edge Fusion:

  • Non-Holonomic Constraints (NHC): Enforces zero-lateral velocity ($v_{\text{lateral}} = 0$).
  • Decoupled Calibration: Phone body axes calibrated with pitch/roll gravity compensation without dependence on compass bearing.
  • HMM Road Network Matching: Bayesian gain schedules vehicle alignment onto OpenStreetMap drivable segments.
  • Zero Velocity Updates (ZUPT): Freezes velocity at standstill (traffic jams) to kill motor vibration drift.
